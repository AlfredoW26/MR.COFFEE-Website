About MR.COFFEE

CEO & Founder

Susanto Liman

Susanto Liman's journey with coffee began during his travels through remote mountain regions, where he discovered the art of coffee cultivation from local farmers who treated each bean like a precious gem. Inspired by their passion and the incredible flavors he experienced, Susanto returned home with a vision: to create a space where exceptional coffee could bring people together.

With a background in hospitality and a heart full of dreams, Susanto opened MR.COFFEE in the heart of the bustling city. His philosophy was simple yet profound - every cup should tell a story, and every customer should feel like they're part of a larger community. Under his leadership, MR.COFFEE has become more than just a coffee shop; it's a sanctuary for dreamers, creatives, and anyone seeking genuine connection.

Susanto personally travels to coffee farms around the world, building relationships with farmers and ensuring that every bean that enters MR.COFFEE meets his exacting standards. His commitment to quality and community has made MR.COFFEE a beloved gathering place where strangers become friends over shared cups of joy.

Our Mission

At MR.COFFEE, our mission is to make all taste coffee every day. We believe that exceptional coffee should be accessible to everyone, and that each cup should be a moment of joy, connection, and inspiration. We are committed to sourcing the finest beans from around the world, supporting sustainable farming practices, and creating a welcoming space where our community can gather, create, and connect.

Our Vision

We envision a world where coffee serves as a bridge between cultures, communities, and individuals. MR.COFFEE aims to be the catalyst for meaningful connections, fostering creativity and inspiration in every corner of our cozy haven. We strive to expand our reach while maintaining the intimate, personal touch that makes every visit special, ensuring that no matter where you are, you can always find a piece of the MR.COFFEE magic.

Our Story

In the heart of a bustling city, there lies MR.COFFEE, a small yet classic coffee shop known for its quirky charm and the aroma of freshly brewed wonders. What started as a dream in Susanto Liman's heart has blossomed into a beloved community gathering place that embodies the spirit of connection and creativity.

Every cup at MR.COFFEE tells a story, crafted with beans sourced from remote mountaintops and brought to life by baristas who seem more like artists. Our team doesn't just serve coffee; they create experiences, turning each interaction into a moment of warmth and genuine care.

The shop, with its mismatched chairs and walls covered in customer doodles, became a cozy haven for dreamers, creatives, and anyone in need of inspiration. These walls have witnessed countless conversations, sparked numerous friendships, and provided comfort to those seeking a moment of peace in their busy lives.

It wasn't just about coffee; it was about connection. MR.COFFEE's charm lives not only in its exceptional brews but in the laughter, the conversations, and the warm smiles that fill the air. Every day, we witness the magic of strangers becoming friends over a shared cup of joy, and this is what drives us to continue our mission of bringing people together, one cup at a time.

As we continue to grow, we remain committed to preserving the intimate, personal atmosphere that makes MR.COFFEE special. Our story is still being written, with each customer adding their own chapter to our ever-expanding tale of community, creativity, and exceptional coffee.
